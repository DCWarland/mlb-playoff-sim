Every remaining matchup

One line per pair of clubs with games left. Leave it on Model and the simulation decides. Choose Rate to fix a win percentage, or Pin to fix an exact result. Either side of a line moves both clubs, because a game one wins is a game the other loses. Overridden lines are shaded. Results read left club first.

Projected standings

Ordered by projected finish, and it re-sorts as you drag — clubs change places and the cut line moves with them. Division leaders are whoever is projected to lead, not whoever leads today. ±Cut is projected wins above or below the third wild card, so a positive number is a club currently forecast to play in October. Opp is the average strength of who a club has left. Projections are exact arithmetic rather than simulated, so they carry no lag; the odds column comes from the simulation and settles a moment behind.

Toronto's road in

A magic number is the oldest arithmetic in baseball: how many combined events must land your way before a result is guaranteed, whatever happens afterwards. Each of your own wins counts one. So does each loss by the club you are chasing. When it reaches zero it is settled. Nothing here is simulated or predicted — it is only games already played, so the sliders have no effect on this tab. It moves once a day, on real results.
